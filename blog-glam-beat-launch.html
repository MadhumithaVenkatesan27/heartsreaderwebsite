Crossed Hearts Announces GLAM BEAT!: A Dedicated Yuri Imprint

2 January 2026

Glam Beat!
Imprint Reveal

Crossed Hearts is excited to announce the launch of GLAM BEAT!, a new publishing imprint created with a singular purpose: to bring girls' love stories, in both print and digital formats, to English-speaking readers worldwide.

Why a Dedicated Yuri Imprint?

Yuri has long been one of the most emotionally rich and versatile genres in comics and prose, yet it has often been treated as a subcategory rather than a genre worthy of its own infrastructure. English releases have historically been scattered, limited in scope, or folded into broader romance lines, making it difficult for readers to consistently discover new titles or explore the full breadth of girls' love storytelling.

GLAM BEAT! was created to address this gap. It is one of the first English-language imprints dedicated entirely to yuri (girls' love).

By dedicating an entire imprint to yuri alone, Crossed Hearts aims to curate a focused, growing catalogue that reflects the genre's diversity while making it easier for fans to find, follow, and collect the stories they love.

A Brief Look at Yuri as a Genre

Yuri, also known as girls' love (GL), encompasses stories that explore emotional, romantic, and intimate relationships between women. The genre has evolved significantly over time, expanding beyond school romance into adult relationships, fantasy, historical settings, psychological drama, and more. GLAM BEAT! exists to bring this full spectrum to English readers in an intentional and consistent way.

What GLAM BEAT! Aims to Publish

GLAM BEAT! is actively exploring a broad range of yuri titles across regions and formats. Our goal is not to define yuri narrowly, but to reflect the many ways girls' love stories are told.

Titles under consideration span a wide variety of sub-genres, including contemporary romance, coming-of-age stories, mature and adult relationships, dramatic and emotionally complex narratives, fantasy and historical yuri, lighthearted and fluffy romances, as well as bold, unconventional, and messy love stories. We are equally interested in quiet introspection and high-intensity emotional arcs, because both are integral to the genre.

Rather than chasing a single trend, GLAM BEAT! is being built as a catalogue that grows over time, allowing readers to explore yuri across moods, themes, and storytelling styles.

Global Reach, Digital and Print

All GLAM BEAT! titles will be released in English, with plans for both digital editions and print releases. Crossed Hearts' existing global distribution network allows us to reach readers across North America, the UK, Europe, Oceania, Southeast Asia, India and beyond, ensuring that English-speaking fans worldwide have access to these stories in official, high-quality editions.

Print remains an important part of this vision. Many yuri readers value physical collections, and GLAM BEAT! is committed to bringing titles to print wherever possible, with formats that respect the original works and appeal to collectors.

Licensing in Progress and Community Input

Licensing discussions for GLAM BEAT! are currently ongoing with multiple publishers, magazines, and rights holders. As an imprint shaped by readers as much as by editors, community recommendations are being carefully reviewed and play an important role in guiding future acquisitions. Announcements will be made as licenses are finalized.

A Long-Term Commitment to Yuri

GLAM BEAT! is not a limited experiment or a short-term label. It represents a long-term commitment to girls' love as a genre with depth, variety, and global relevance. By giving yuri its own dedicated imprint, Crossed Hearts aims to support creators, expand accessibility for readers, and contribute meaningfully to the growth of English-language yuri publishing.

This is only the beginning. More updates and announcements will be shared soon.
