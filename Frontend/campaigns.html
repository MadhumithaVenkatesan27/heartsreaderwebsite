Website-Exclusive Special Editions

Every title below is a limited, website-exclusive Special Edition. Reserve yours now at the standard price. Once an edition is fully reserved, it moves to general retail. Early reservers keep the price they paid.

How It Works

Reserving your Special Edition

Four quick steps. Reserve early, lock in your price, and be first in line when production begins.

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Log in or create a free account so your reservation is saved to you.

  2. 2

    Pick your edition

    Choose a title below and press Reserve This Edition. Each one includes its own exclusive extra, like a photocard set, bookmark, or shikishi.

  3. 3

    Add your details

    Enter your name, email, and shipping address so we know where to send your copy.

  4. 4

    Confirm & pay securely

    Pay by card through Stripe. Once it goes through, your copy is reserved and you'll see the progress bar move.

Your price is locked in Reserve now at the standard price. Even when the edition later moves to retail, you keep the price you paid.
Goal-based editions Each edition has a reservation goal shown on its progress bar. Once it's fully reserved, it moves to general retail.
