Announcement Blush Club!

Blush Club Announces Its First Four Titles

June 2026  ·  Dallas, TX  ·  For Immediate Release

Blush Club
Debut Lineup Reveal

Crossed Hearts' Boys' Love Imprint Reveals Debut Lineup Spanning Fantasy, Supernatural Romance and Heartfelt Drama

Blush Club, the Boys' Love imprint of Crossed Hearts — a global publishing house specializing in the English localization and international distribution of Japanese manga, Korean webcomics and Asian novels — is proud to announce its debut publishing slate: four original yaoi titles releasing simultaneously on December 1, 2026. Spanning warrior epics, divine courts, supernatural school life and quiet campus longing, the lineup reflects the full emotional range that has made Boys' Love one of the most beloved genres in comics and fiction today.

Each title is authored and illustrated by its original creator and will be available in standard print, limited edition print and e-book formats at the following list prices: $12.99 USD (standard and limited print) and $7.00 USD (e-book).

What Is Blush Club?

Blush Club is Crossed Hearts' imprint dedicated to Boys' Love (BL) — stories built around romantic and emotional relationships between men. Rather than sticking to one tone or formula, Blush Club's lineup spans a wide range of genre and mood: drama, comedy, fantasy and slice-of-life, all united by a focus on intimacy, longing, and connections that feel personal, heartfelt, and quietly powerful. Welcome to Blush Club, a place where stories of love come alive. Stay tuned, stay passionate, and stay ready to fall in love.

The Debut Lineup

The Bird in the Cage Dreams
Art & Story: Hirakai Yakei
"I cursed you with one command: live."

Born into the bloodline destined to lead the war-loving Raiveda Clan, Jiz has spent his life working toward a single goal: freeing Tsado, a retired warrior held captive on the outskirts of their settlement. To everyone else, Tsado is a disgraced veteran. To Jiz, he is something far greater — a battle-hardened equal, a trusted comrade, and the man who has quietly claimed a place in his heart. But before Jiz can act on his feelings, Tsado murders one of the clan's most powerful elders, and their shared future fractures in an instant. Branded a criminal and sentenced to death, Tsado becomes the enemy of the people he once protected. Now, with the tribe demanding justice and destiny pulling them to opposite ends, Jiz must choose between the chieftain's seat he has spent his life earning and the man he refuses to let go.

A dark, sweeping historical BL epic

Two warriors bound by love, separated by betrayal, and cursed to keep living when loss might be kinder.

The King of Owls and His Troubled Attendant
Art & Story: Chikuwabu Tanno

In the divine realm of Kakuriyo, King Madara of the Northern Bird Clan is everyone's problem — carefree, commitment-averse, and spectacularly uninterested in finding a destined mate. No one knows this better than Sagari, his loyal attendant and childhood friend, who has spent years managing the king's chaos while quietly carrying a love he knows he can never confess. They are friends, yes — but they are also master and servant, and that line does not move. Then one unexpected night changes everything. When Madara begins pursuing Sagari with a warmth and persistence that is impossible to dismiss, the careful walls Sagari has built around his heart start coming apart. Now the question is no longer whether the king can be serious — it's whether a devoted attendant can dare to imagine a future where they stand as equals.

A fantasy BL romance

Built on slow-burn tension, devotion tested by duty, and the courage it takes to reach across a lifetime of careful distance.

Until We Fall in Love
Art & Story: An Chanoki

Aito has one rule: keep his identity secret. As a cupid quietly working among humans, his job is to guide others toward love — a task made considerably harder by the fact that he doesn't entirely understand what love feels like himself. Then Kowata, the school's most popular and openly affectionate boy, confesses to him with complete sincerity. Aito brushes him off. Kowata comes back. Aito brushes him off again. Kowata, undeterred, simply keeps showing up — warm, persistent, and armed with a smile that even a cupid finds difficult to ignore. As Aito works to protect his secret while carrying out his duties, he discovers that the hardest hearts to understand are not the ones he's been assigned to — it's his own.

A sweet, lightly supernatural high school BL romance

About the cupid who may be the last to understand what his own arrow is doing to him.

A Gaze Like Lightning
Art & Story: Yuhi Shinome

Hiyori already knows how this ends. Ryo is handsome, well-liked, quietly magnetic — and thoroughly out of reach for someone as ordinary as Hiyori. So when Ryo's friends ask him to play matchmaker, he agrees, because that's the kind of person Hiyori is: kind, self-effacing, always willing to help with the thing that quietly breaks his heart. After a shift at their shared part-time job, he asks Ryo, almost casually, if he has a girlfriend. "No. But there is someone I like." Hiyori braces himself. "It's you."

A warm, quietly devastating college BL romance

About the specific pain of loving someone you're convinced you can't have — and the moment that changes everything.

Availability & Distribution

All four Blush Club debut titles release on December 1, 2026 in standard print ($12.99 USD), limited edition print ($12.99 USD) and e-book ($7.00 USD) formats.

Digital editions will be available across Amazon, Apple Books, Google Books, Barnes & Noble, BookWalker and Manga Plaza. Print editions will be distributed through Publisher's Group West (Ingram) across the USA, Canada and Latin America; Simon & Schuster across Australia and New Zealand; and directly through Crossed Hearts in India and the Middle East.

About Blush Club

Blush Club is Crossed Hearts' dedicated Boys' Love imprint, publishing yaoi, Korean manhwa BL, Chinese danmei, and BL novels for readers worldwide. Blush Club is built around Boys' Love that explores intimacy, longing and connections that feel personal, heartfelt and quietly powerful.

About Crossed Hearts

Crossed Hearts is a global publishing house dedicated to the English-language localization and international distribution of Japanese manga, Korean webcomics and Asian novels. Every title in the Crossed Hearts catalogue is a story the team believes in. Crossed Hearts has 40 print volumes scheduled for release in 2026.
