Common questions

Everything worth knowing before you subscribe.

What's the difference between manga only and all formats?
Manga only keeps your picks to our manga catalogue at a lower price. All formats lets you pick manga, manhwa or novels each cycle — better value if you read across formats.
Will I get the limited or regular edition?
Whichever tier you're on, you'll get the limited edition if the title is open for pre-order, or the regular edition once it has launched.
What happens if I skip a month?
Your credit carries forward automatically. Credits accumulate for as long as you're subscribed, so you can save several up and redeem them together when a title you've been waiting for arrives.
Is the bonus manga random?
Yes — on both annual plans it's a surprise pick from our catalogue, chosen by our editorial team. It won't duplicate a title you've already selected.
When am I charged?
Monthly plans charge on the same date each month, annual plans once upfront. Payments are automatic and you'll get a receipt by email.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next billing date. Annual plans can be cancelled but are non-refundable after the first 14 days. Any credits you haven't used remain valid for 12 months after cancellation, then expire.
Is this available outside the USA?
The program is currently United States only. We're working on international expansion — join the waitlist to hear when your region launches.
